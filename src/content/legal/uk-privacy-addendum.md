# UK Privacy Addendum

*This addendum applies in addition to our main Privacy Policy, specifically for learners based in the United Kingdom. Read it alongside, not instead of, the main policy.*

## UK GDPR

Following the UK's departure from the EU, the UK operates its own version of the GDPR (the "UK GDPR"), alongside the Data Protection Act 2018, sitting separately from the EU's GDPR. We treat UK learners' personal information in line with UK GDPR requirements, which are substantively similar to the EU regime described in our main Privacy Policy.
