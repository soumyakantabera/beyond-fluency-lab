# US Privacy Addendum

*This addendum applies in addition to our main Privacy Policy, specifically for learners based in the United States. Read it alongside, not instead of, the main policy.*

## Overview

The United States does not have one single federal privacy law. Instead, a growing number of states (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and others) have their own privacy laws with broadly similar consumer rights. This addendum summarizes how those rights apply to our services, regardless of which US state you're in.

## Categories of personal information we collect

As described in our main Privacy Policy: identity/contact details, Plateau Diagnostic responses, course and enrolment details, class attendance and recordings, payment status (not full card numbers), and basic technical/log data.

## We do not sell or share your personal information

Under California's CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws, "sale" and "share" of personal information are defined broadly, including some forms of advertising-related data sharing. **We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.** We do not use tracking pixels or ad networks on our site.

## Your rights (California and similar states)

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to:

- **Know** what personal information we've collected about you and why
- **Delete** personal information we hold about you, subject to certain exceptions (such as records we're required to keep for accounting or tax purposes)
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information
- **Opt out** of sale or sharing of personal information (not applicable here, since we don't sell or share it)
- **Non-discrimination** — we will not charge you a different price or provide a different level of service because you exercised any of these rights

To exercise any of these rights, email info@learnwithsmile.app. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

## Sensitive personal information

We do not collect sensitive personal information (such as government ID numbers, precise geolocation, or health information) beyond what's incidentally needed to process a payment through our payment providers, who handle that data directly under their own compliance obligations.

## Data controller

The entity responsible for your personal information (referred to as the "business" under most US state privacy laws) is Learn With Smile, a sole proprietorship in India, trading as Beyond Fluency Lab — see the main Privacy Policy for full contact details.

## Retention

See the "How long we keep information" section of our main Privacy Policy — the same retention approach applies to US learners.
