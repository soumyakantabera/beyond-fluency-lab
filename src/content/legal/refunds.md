# Refund & Cancellation Policy

*Last updated: 11 September 2026*

*This page describes how Beyond Fluency Lab handles cancellations and refunds. It does not reduce any statutory right you have under the law of your own country, which applies regardless of anything written here.*

## Free trial

Free trial classes carry no payment and no obligation. Cancel or reschedule a trial any time before it starts, at no cost, by contacting info@learnwithsmile.app.

## Your right to cancel — EU and UK learners

If you are a consumer based in the European Union or United Kingdom, you generally have a legal right to cancel a distance contract for services (including online courses) within **14 days** of enrolling, without giving a reason, and to receive a full refund.

**How this applies to our courses, specifically:**

- If you enrol and your course has **not yet started**, you can cancel within 14 days for a full refund, no questions asked.
- Because our courses run on a live group schedule, your course may start (your first live session may occur) before the 14-day period ends. **If you want to start attending live sessions immediately, before the 14-day period has passed, we will ask you to expressly acknowledge and agree that you are requesting early performance and understand that once the course is fully delivered, you lose the right to cancel.** You will see this acknowledgment as a clear checkbox at checkout — it is not buried in these terms.
- If you agree to early performance and then cancel partway through the 14-day period, before the course is fully delivered, you are entitled to a refund for the portion of the course not yet delivered, calculated proportionally against the sessions or weeks already provided.
- After the 14-day period has passed, or once the course has been fully delivered (whichever is earlier), the statutory cancellation right no longer applies, and our standard cancellation terms below apply instead.

## Standard cancellation terms (after the statutory withdrawal period, or for learners outside the EU/UK)

- **Before the course starts:** full refund, minus any payment-processing fee already incurred, if you cancel at least 48 hours before your first scheduled session.
- **After the course starts:** refunds are calculated proportionally based on sessions not yet delivered, minus a reasonable administrative fee, provided you cancel with reasonable notice.
- **No-shows:** missed sessions without advance notice are not eligible for refund or make-up, though we will always try to accommodate a genuine emergency — contact us and we'll work with you.

## Rescheduling

If you need to switch to a different group or time slot rather than cancel, contact us before your course starts — we will do our best to accommodate this at no extra cost, subject to availability.

## How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method, through the same payment provider (Stripe or Razorpay) used for the original payment, and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card issuer. Any tax collected via Stripe Tax on the original payment is refunded alongside the course fee where the refund is for the full amount, or adjusted proportionally for partial refunds.

## Removal for conduct reasons

As set out in our Terms of Service, a learner removed from a course due to abusive behaviour or serious disruption is not entitled to a refund for sessions already delivered up to that point.

## How to request a cancellation or refund

Email info@learnwithsmile.app with your name, the course you enrolled in, and your reason for cancelling (required for statutory EU/UK withdrawal requests only if asked; not required for our standard policy). We aim to respond within 2 business days.
