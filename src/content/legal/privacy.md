## What this policy covers

This policy applies to beyondfluencylab.com (or its current domain) and to personal information you give us when you take the Plateau Diagnostic, enquire, book a free trial, enrol, pay, or attend a class. It does not cover websites or payment pages we don't control, including Stripe, Razorpay, and any messaging platform you contact us through.

## Information we collect

- **Identity and contact:** name, email address, country, and preferred class slot
- **Plateau Diagnostic responses:** your answers to the assessment, and the plateau type it identifies for you, if you choose to receive your result by email
- **Course details:** the programme you enquire about, goals you mention, and notes needed to place you in a suitable group
- **Class delivery:** attendance, trainer feedback, and recordings of live sessions for enrolled learners to revise
- **Payments:** amount, date, course, and payment status. Card and bank details are collected and processed directly by our payment providers (Stripe and/or Razorpay). We do not store full card numbers ourselves.
- **Technical data:** basic server logs such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, kept by our hosting provider to operate and secure the site

## How we use information

We use personal information to reply to your enquiry, send your Plateau Diagnostic result, confirm a trial or enrolment, deliver live classes, share recordings with enrolled learners in that group, process payments, and keep records we reasonably need for accounting, tax, and dispute handling.

**We do not sell personal information**, and we do not use it to run advertising networks or third-party marketing lists.

## Legal basis for processing (EU/UK/wherever applicable)

Where data protection law requires a stated legal basis, we rely on: your consent (for example, taking the Plateau Diagnostic or subscribing to updates), performance of a contract (once you enrol in a course), our legitimate interest in running and securing the website, and legal obligations (such as tax and accounting record-keeping).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, contact us at info@learnwithsmile.app. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (for example, one month under GDPR), and may need to verify your identity first.

If you are in the EU or UK and are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

*See the UK Privacy Addendum and US Privacy Addendum for rights specific to those jurisdictions.*

## Payments

Fees are collected through Stripe and/or Razorpay, depending on your location and payment method. Each provider's own privacy policy applies to the data you enter directly on their checkout page. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded or failed, along with basic transaction details — not your full card number.

## Class recordings

Live classes are recorded so enrolled learners in that group can revise or catch up. Recordings are for personal study by learners in that group only. They are not posted publicly or used for marketing without your separate, specific permission. If you do not wish to appear in a recording, tell us before the session so we can discuss a practical arrangement.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies strictly necessary to operate the site (for example, remembering your cookie preference itself) unless you separately consent to additional analytics or marketing cookies. See our [Cookie Policy] for the current list and how to manage your preferences.

## Who we share information with

We share information only with people and providers who help us deliver the service:

- Trainers and operations staff who run your group
- Stripe and/or Razorpay, for payment processing
- Our website hosting provider
- Professional advisers or authorities where the law requires it, or to protect learners, staff, or the public

We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not share it with data brokers.

## International data transfers

Because we operate from India and serve learners across the EU, UK, and other countries, your personal information may be transferred to and processed in India. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for transfers of EU/UK personal data outside the EU/UK. Contact us for details of the specific safeguard used for your data.

## How long we keep information

Enquiry messages that don't lead to enrolment are kept only as long as needed to reply and for a short follow-up period. Enrolment, attendance, and payment records are kept for the duration of the course and for a reasonable period afterward for accounting, tax, and dispute purposes, then deleted or anonymised unless the law requires a longer retention period.

## Children and young people

Our programmes are designed for university students, graduates, and working professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information without appropriate consent, contact us and we will delete it where we reasonably can.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information. No website, payment provider, or messaging platform is perfectly secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices or the law change. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest version. Continued use of the site or our classes after an update means the new version applies going forward.
