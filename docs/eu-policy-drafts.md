## Cancellation & Refund Policy

### Free trials

A free trial requires no payment and creates no obligation to enrol. Contact us if you need a different time.

### Withdrawal from an online service contract

EU consumers normally have 14 days from the day after the service contract is concluded to withdraw without giving a reason. Tell us clearly at [email] or [postal address] before the deadline. You may use the form below, but it is not compulsory.

If you expressly request sessions during that period, we explain the consequences beforehand. Where legally permitted, a withdrawal may require payment proportionate to coaching already supplied. The right ends after full performance only when the required prior consent and acknowledgement have been obtained. Attending a first session does not automatically remove it. Any longer rights arising from missing information or national law remain protected.

### Additional cancellation promise — proposed business policy

After the statutory period, you may cancel future sessions by writing to us. We refund the unused portion of your course fee, calculated using the agreed number of sessions. Sessions already delivered or missed before we receive your cancellation are not included in this additional promise. Statutory remedies for a deficient service are unaffected.

### Rescheduling and provider cancellation

Ask about rescheduling as soon as possible. Group catch-up options depend on availability and are not guaranteed. If we cancel a session, you can accept an alternative or receive a refund for that session. If we cancel the remaining course, we refund all undelivered sessions. You do not have to accept a credit instead.

### Refund processing

Statutory withdrawal refunds are made without undue delay and within 14 days after notice, subject only to lawful deductions. We use the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise, without a refund fee. We also process refunds under our additional cancellation promise within 14 days. Bank posting times may vary.
